## Preparation Timeline

Croatia's Fiscalization Act (Zakon o fiskalizaciji, NN 89/25, June 2025, in force September 1, 2025) introduces a combined transaction control / real-time reporting model for B2B e-invoices. This is not a clearance model - invoices are issued first and reported simultaneously to the Tax Administration's CIS (Centralni informacijski sustav). The unique identifiers JIR (Jedinstveni identifikator racuna - assigned by CIS) and ZKI (Zastitni kod izdavatelja - a hash generated by the issuer's private key) must appear on every invoice.

Phase 1 applies to VAT-registered entities (PDV obveznici) from January 1, 2026. All entities must be capable of receiving e-invoices from the same date. Phase 2 extends the obligation to non-VAT-registered businesses from January 1, 2027.

Foreign entities with a Croatian VAT ID (OIB) are in scope, including non-established entities.

For a group implementing Croatian e-invoicing from a standing start:

**FINA access point setup (4-6 weeks).** FINA (Financijska agencija) is the Croatian Peppol Authority and the primary B2B/B2G transport platform. Onboarding requires entity verification, certificate procurement for QES (Qualified Electronic Signature - required for B2B signatures in Croatia), and test submission via the FINA demo portal.

**JIR/ZKI integration (8-12 weeks).** The ZKI is a cryptographic hash generated by the issuer's private key before the invoice is submitted to CIS. CIS then returns the JIR. Both must be embedded in the invoice and in the QR code. Standard ERP billing engines have no ZKI generation or JIR retrieval logic. Custom connector development is required for every ERP implementation.

**OIB master data scrubbing (3-5 weeks).** Every invoice requires the 11-digit OIB of both issuer and recipient. Standard ERP customer/vendor records may store only VAT numbers or OIBs with incorrect digit counts. Full scrubbing across the Croatian customer/vendor portfolio is required before go-live.

**Minimum:** 12-16 weeks with a certified integrator and single entity. **Stretched:** 20-30 weeks for multi-entity groups or where ZKI/JIR connector development is done in-house.

## Operational Ownership

**Finance Systems** owns the ERP connector for JIR/ZKI generation and the HR-FISK 2.0 format output. The billing engine must: generate the ZKI (issuer hash), submit to CIS, receive the JIR, embed both in the invoice, and generate the QR code containing the JIR. All of this happens before the invoice reaches the recipient. Additionally, QES (Qualified Electronic Signature) is required for B2B transactions - Finance Systems must manage the signing certificate lifecycle.

**Tax/Compliance** owns scope determination: which Croatian entities are VAT-registered (Phase 1) vs. non-VAT-registered (Phase 2), whether any transactions are exempt, and the 48-hour offline fallback procedure. Tax also owns archiving: 11 years under Zakon o racunovodstvu (NN 85/24 Art. 10) for accounting records. The Fiscalization Act sets 6 years for fiscal records (NN 89/25 Art. 35(1)) - both periods must be maintained if they apply to the same records.

**Master Data/IT** owns OIB scrubbing: validating 11-digit OIBs for all Croatian customers and vendors, ensuring the OIB is stored in a dedicated field in ERP (not overloaded onto the VAT number field), and maintaining OIB data quality as new Croatian partners are onboarded.

**IT** owns FINA access point registration, QES certificate procurement and renewal, CIS API integration, and the 48-hour offline fallback queue. Offline invoices issued during a CIS outage must be reported to CIS within 48 hours of restoration.

**Where it breaks:** The JIR/ZKI integration gap. The hash-and-retrieve cycle required before invoice issuance is architecturally different from standard billing engine flows. Groups that scope Croatian compliance as a format change discover during development that the ERP cannot generate ZKI hashes or call CIS without a custom connector. This gap is found late in every implementation that does not run an ERP footprint audit first.

The configuration work items in each cluster vary by ERP system, entity structure, and current baseline. That specificity is what the Readiness Sprint delivers.

## Data & Infrastructure

**OIB as universal identifier.** The OIB (11-digit) is required on all invoices for both issuer and recipient. It is Croatia's universal tax and company identifier. Unlike other EU countries where VAT number and company registration number are separate, the OIB serves both functions. ERP customer/vendor master fields that hold only VAT numbers (with HR prefix) must be supplemented with a dedicated OIB field.

**JIR and ZKI - non-standard invoice fields.** HR-FISK 2.0 extends EN 16931 with mandatory Croatian fields: JIR (UUID from Tax Administration CIS), ZKI (cryptographic hash from issuer private key), KPD code (payment type), and a QR code. None of these fields exist in standard EN 16931 UBL 2.1 or CII. ERP output must be extended to include all four.

**QES requirement.** Croatian law requires a Qualified Electronic Signature on B2B e-invoices. This is unusual in the EU context (most countries do not mandate QES for B2B). The certificate must be tied to the issuer's OIB. Certificate procurement from a qualified trust service provider, integration with the ERP signing module, and lifecycle management (renewal before expiry) are all in scope.

**Archiving split.** The Fiscalization Act (NN 89/25 Art. 35(1)) requires 6-year retention for fiscal records from year-end of invoice issuance. The Accounting Act (NN 85/24 Art. 10) requires 11-year retention for accounting records. Both may apply to the same invoice. The longer period (11 years) should be applied to avoid a gap.

**FINA sandbox.** demo-pkiservis.fina.hr and the Porezna demo portal provide test environment access. Full JIR/ZKI cycle testing should be completed in the sandbox before production go-live.

## The Friction Map

**JIR/ZKI connector absent from ERP.** The hash-generate-then-retrieve cycle for ZKI and JIR is a non-trivial API integration. Standard SAP, Oracle, and Microsoft ERP products have no HR-FISK 2.0 connector. Custom middleware is required. Groups that treat Croatian compliance as a Peppol format extension find the ZKI/JIR requirement only when they try to map the invoice fields.

**QES certificate gap.** QES is required for B2B signatures - this is unusual in EU B2B e-invoicing. IT departments that have not procured or integrated QES certificates discover this requirement during legal review, not during ERP configuration. Certificate procurement from a Croatian qualified trust service provider takes 4-6 weeks minimum.

**Phase-in resolution.** The phase-in boundary between VAT-registered (January 2026) and non-VAT-registered (January 2027) requires entity-by-entity scoping. A Croatian group entity that is registered for income tax but not VAT may have a different deadline than its VAT-registered subsidiaries. The entity-level scoping exercise is the first step of any readiness review.

Every group has a version of at least one of these. Finding which ones, and in which subsidiaries, is how a Readiness Sprint starts.
